Questions

Which Odoo setups work?

Odoo on your own server (Community or Enterprise), where AskaDB can connect to the PostgreSQL database with a read-only login. Odoo Online (SaaS) does not give database access, so it is not supported. The reports are tested on Odoo 18.

Is my data safe?

AskaDB only reads. Every query is checked and rolled back. Only table and column names go to the AI, never your data rows. Reports and emails are made on your PC and sent from your own mailbox. See the privacy policy.

What happens after the trial?

The trial starts when you first open an Odoo database. After 14 days the Report Center, comparisons and Report Center emails need Pro. Asking questions, exports and saved questions stay free.

Can I cancel?

Yes, any time, from your Gumroad receipt. Pro keeps working until the end of the period you paid for.

Can I pay in Egyptian pounds or by bank transfer?

Yes for companies in Egypt and the Gulf. Book a demo and we will send an invoice.
